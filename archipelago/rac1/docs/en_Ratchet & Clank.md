# Ratchet & Clank

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a config
file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every gadget and item the story gives (the Swingshot, the Heli-Pack, the Hoverboard, the Magneboots ...), the infobots that
open new planets, the special items (Zoomerator, Raritanium, Codebot, Premium and Ultra Nanotech) and the 40 gold bolts are
shuffled into the multiworld. With an option, so are the weapons the Gadgetron vendors sell.

## What is the goal of Ratchet & Clank when randomized?

Defeat Chairman Drek on Veldin.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Gadgets, items, weapons, planet coordinates (the infobots), special items and gold bolts.

## What does another world's item look like in Ratchet & Clank?

The game's own object stays where it is (the infobot, the gadget, the gold bolt); picking it up sends the item, and a line at the
bottom of the screen says what was sent to whom.

## What versions of the game are supported?

Ratchet & Clank played in [rac-rs-multiplayer](https://github.com/adamlbunch/rac-rs-multiplayer) (the PC port of the PS2 game),
from your own disc (USA or PAL). PCSX2 and the PS3/PS4 versions are not supported.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

The game gives it as it would: a gadget is in your inventory, a planet appears on the ship's map, gold bolts count up. A line at
the bottom of the screen says what you got and from whom.

## What else is different from the vanilla game?

- You start with Novalis and a few more early planets on the ship's map (option).
- Holding `L1 + L2 + R1 + R2 + Select` restarts the planet you are on at its landing pad, if you are stuck.
- Gold bolts count what the multiworld sent you; the gold bolts lying in the levels are locations.
- Co-op: two players of a multiplayer session can play one slot together (both run the client on the same slot); what one finds
  counts for both, and both games get every item.
